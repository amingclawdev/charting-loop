# Optional authority evidence

Authority is external to the three-plane corridor loop and optional for a minimal v2
Candidate or ordinary neutral run. No directory, filename, transcript, schema, digest,
or completeness claim is required. Absence means authority behavior is `unobserved`
and AuthorityWarranty is `not_assessed` or `unknown`; it proves neither permission nor
prohibition.

An authority log is a format-free reminder, not a protocol record. A study may retain a
consent-aware opaque pointer, but neither presence nor format participates in Candidate
validation, ordinary study participation, baseline comparison, or Warranty issuance.
An independent AuthorityWarranty record—not this log—must bind issuer, authority
source, actor–role Assignment, act, exact target/version, scope, WorldRef, governing
Rule, lifecycle, provenance, and conflict resolver.

An AI may diagnose, draft, and record authority material. It cannot create real-world
authority, ratify its own Candidate, or use a path certificate as authority. Extending an
authority log never extends a closed WorldSpan or mints a Warranty.

Collect only the declared session with permission. Never publish credentials, hidden
reasoning, unrelated account history, or unlicensed third-party private data. Private
raw material belongs in ignored `exogenous/local/`. Public attachments are opt-in and
opaque to the core validator.
